White to move. White has two knights (a4 and b3) and a bishop on c4 against Black's knight on g4 and aggressively placed pawns. Black's queen is on d8 and king on f8. What is the clearest winning plan?

A Plan A — Queen check: Qa8+
Play Qa8+ Ke7 and win the queen on d8 with discovered attack.
B Plan B — Knight capture: Nxb5
Win a pawn with Nxb5 and improve material count heading into the endgame.
C Plan C — Bishop activity: Bd5
Centralize the bishop to d5 to dominate the center and restrict Black's knights.
✓ Correct — A is the best plan.
✗ Not quite. The answer is Plan A.

Why Plan A?

Plan A (Qa8+!) is a queen check that wins the queen. After Qa8+ Ke7 (forced), White plays Qxd8+ Kxd8 and White has traded queens while winning a full piece (or the reverse — the Black queen on d8 is captured). White is then ahead a piece in a simplified endgame.

Plan B (Nxb5) wins only a pawn — far less than a queen. Plan C (Bd5) is fine positionally but ignores the immediate queen win available.

Lesson: Always look for forcing moves that win major material before playing positional improvements.
