A planning workout built from real Titled Tuesday games. You see the position, read three candidate plans, then choose the one that fits the position best.

**How it works:**

- A chess board shows you the position from the game
- Three plans (A, B, C) are described in words — not moves
- Click the plan you believe is best
- Instantly see whether you were right and read a full explanation

The goal is not to calculate — it's to **recognize the right type of plan** for the position. That pattern recognition is what separates strong players from average ones.
