A planning workout built from real Titled Tuesday games. You see the position, read three candidate plans, then choose the one that fits the position best.

- A chess board shows the position from the game
- Three plans (A, B, C) are described in words — not moves
- Click the plan you believe is best
- See immediately whether you were right, with a full explanation

The goal is not to calculate — it is to **recognise the right type of plan** for the position.
