Each session pulls 40 positions from one Titled Tuesday tournament, grouped into four phases:

- **Transition to Middlegame** — the first moment plans diverge
- **Middlegame** — piece coordination, pawn structure, open files
- **Transition to Endgame** — when and how to simplify
- **Endgame** — technique, king activation, passed pawns

Work through all four phases to complete a session. Progress is saved automatically.
