Each session covers 40 positions pulled from one Titled Tuesday tournament. Positions are grouped into four phases of the game:

- **Transition to Middlegame** — the first critical moment when plans diverge
- **Middlegame** — open battles, pawn structures, piece coordination
- **Transition to Endgame** — recognising when to simplify and how
- **Endgame** — technique, king activation, passed pawns

Work through all four phases to complete a session. Your progress is saved automatically.
